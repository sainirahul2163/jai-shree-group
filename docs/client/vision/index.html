Buyers search for one product in one city: “laser cutting job work Pune”, “expanded metal Mumbai”. A specialist site answers that search far better than one page on a general site.

The idea

One group. Seven websites. One engine underneath.

The big idea

Seven shop windows, one factory behind them

What the buyer sees

Seven different websites, each with its own name, colours, products, phone number and a free tool built for its buyers. Every unit site carries the mark “A Jai Shree Group company”.

What we run

One system. One login to manage every site's enquiries, photos and blog. One place to fix a bug or add a feature — and all seven sites get it the same day.

Think of a CNC turret punch. One machine, many tools. Change the tool and you get a different hole — without buying a new machine. We build one engine and give each unit its own “tool”.

Challenges and how
we overcome them

Every plan has risks. Here is each one we have found, and what we have already put in place.

Challenges → solutions

What could go wrong, and what we do about it

Google may see similar sites as copies
→ Every site gets its own words, its own angle and its own searches. Shared design, never shared paragraphs.
Two live unit sites could lose their Google rankings
→ Every old page is mapped to its new page and forwarded permanently (38 pages + 57 images already mapped).
Building could break the live main site
→ Work happens on a separate copy. Before going live we compared 80 screenshots of 40 pages — no unintended change.
Missing content: unit photos, logos, stock list
→ We launch with safe fallbacks (group mark + unit name, product photos). We never invent specs, stock or clients.
Enquiries going to the wrong person
→ Each site carries its unit's phone and email. Every enquiry is tagged with its site and routed automatically.
Buyers confused about who is who
→ Every unit site says “A Jai Shree Group company”; the group's Our Group page links to all six.
Enquiry data in the wrong hands
→ Admin access is locked to approved accounts; uploaded drawings will be kept in private storage. Privacy and terms pages follow India's DPDP Act 2023.
Seven sites = seven times the upkeep?
→ No: one engine. One update, one security patch, one hosting bill for all seven.

Protecting what already works

Old pages get a forwarding address

shreeperforators.com and metalperforatedsheets.com have been on Google for years. When the new sites go live, every old page sends its visitors — and its Google credit — to the matching new page.

Pages for products the group no longer makes (like wire mesh) forward to the products page — never to a page offering them.
